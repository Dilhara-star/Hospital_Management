Privacy Policy

How Medi Plus collects, uses, and protects your information.

  2. Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Medi Plus collects when you use our website and appointment booking system, and how that information is used and protected.

1. Information We Collect

When you register, book an appointment, or contact us, we may collect information such as your name, phone number, email address, date of birth, gender, and any medical or appointment details you provide. We also collect payment details when you pay for a consultation or medicine order, through our payment provider.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to create and manage your account, book and manage your appointments, process payments and refunds, send appointment reminders and updates, and respond to your questions through our contact form.

3. Sharing of Information

We share your information with the doctors and staff involved in your care, and with payment providers to process your payments. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

4. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, including keeping passwords stored in a secure, encrypted form. However, no method of storage or transmission over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Your Choices

You can view and update most of your personal information at any time from your Profile page. If you would like your account or data removed, please contact us using the details on our Contact page.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

7. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach us using the details on our Contact page.
